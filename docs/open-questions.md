# Open questions

1. ~~**Launch market.**~~ Decided: **United Kingdom**.
2. **Identity provider.** Which scheme to use, and how to avoid storing sensitive identity data directly?
3. **Session re-verification.** Is a passkey or biometric unlock enough each session, or is a fresh identity check needed periodically?
4. **Business model.** Advertising rewards engagement, which conflicts with the mission. Options: subscriptions for organisations, public or civic funding, non-profit or foundation structure.
5. **Moderation.** Who chairs debates (staff, elected volunteers, officials)? How are appeals handled?
6. **Political neutrality.** How is the platform kept, and seen to be, non-partisan (governance, an independent board, published rules)?
7. **Officials' obligations.** Should official participation be voluntary, or encouraged through partnerships with legislatures?
8. **Data protection and security.** Political opinion is special-category data under GDPR, and a verified political platform is a high-value target.
9. ~~**Platform.**~~ Decided: **web first**, mobile later.
